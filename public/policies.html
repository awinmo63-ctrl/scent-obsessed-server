Shipping & Delivery Policy

At Scent Obsessed we are committed to delivering your signature fragrances in the safest and most efficient manner. Each bottle is hand-inspected and securely packaged to ensure it reaches you in pristine condition.

1. Order processing & dispatch

All orders are processed and dispatched within 24–48 hours, excluding Sundays and public holidays. You will receive an email and SMS notification once your order is confirmed, and again when it is handed to our delivery partners (Shiprocket / Blue Dart / Delhivery).

2. Estimated delivery timelines

Due to safety regulations, perfumes are usually transported by surface shipping.

DestinationEstimated delivery
Punjab & North India2–4 business days
Metros (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, etc.)3–6 business days
Rest of India5–8 business days
Remote areas / North East7–10 business days

3. Shipping charges

A flat shipping fee of ₹99 applies to orders below ₹499. Shipping is free on all orders above ₹499.

4. Real-time tracking

Once dispatched you will receive a unique tracking ID. You can track your package through our website or the courier partner's tracking portal.

5. Packaging & handling

Your fragrance is housed in high-quality glass. To prevent damage, every bottle is encased in shock-absorbent padding, packages are sealed with tamper-evident tape, and outer boxes are marked "Fragile: Handle with Care".

6. Damaged or missing shipments

Mandatory unboxing video. To claim a replacement for a broken or leaking bottle, we require a continuous, unedited unboxing video showing the package being opened for the first time.

Report any damage or discrepancy within 24 hours of delivery via WhatsApp on 78149 30720 or by email to info@scentobsessed.in.

7. Delivery attempts & RTO

Our courier partners make up to three delivery attempts. If delivery is unsuccessful after three attempts the package is Returned to Origin (RTO). Re-shipping charges may apply for orders returned due to an incorrect address or unavailability.

8. Cash on Delivery

COD is available for most PIN codes across India. A nominal COD handling fee of ₹49 may be charged at checkout to cover additional processing costs.

Refunds & Returns Policy

Thank you for choosing Scent Obsessed. We take immense pride in the quality and craftsmanship of our fragrances. Because our products are personal care items, our return policy is designed to ensure the safety and satisfaction of all our customers.

1. Returns & exchanges

  • Unopened products: returns or exchanges are accepted only for products in their original, unopened and sealed packaging within 7 days of delivery.
  • Opened products: due to the nature of fragrances and hygiene standards, we cannot accept returns or exchanges for bottles that have been opened, sprayed, or had the cellophane seal removed.
  • Discovery sets: discovery and sample sets are final sale and cannot be returned.

2. Damaged or wrong items

If you receive a bottle that is broken or leaking, or the wrong fragrance:

  • Mandatory unboxing video — a continuous video showing the package being opened for the first time is required to process a damage claim.
  • Timeline — report the issue within 24 hours of delivery to info@scentobsessed.in or WhatsApp 78149 30720.
  • Resolution — once verified, we provide a free replacement or a full refund at no extra cost to you.

3. Refunds

Once your unopened return is received and inspected we will notify you of approval or rejection. Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are issued as store credit or by bank transfer to an account you provide.

4. Return shipping costs

If the return is due to a mistake on our part (damaged or wrong item), Scent Obsessed bears the return shipping cost. For change-of-mind returns of unopened items, the customer is responsible for return shipping fees.

5. Cancellations

You may cancel your order as long as it has not yet been dispatched. Once the order is with our delivery partner (Shiprocket / Blue Dart) it cannot be cancelled.
