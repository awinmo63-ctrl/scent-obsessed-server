Shipping & Delivery

Shipping is complimentary on every order across India. There is no minimum order value.

Dispatch

Orders are dispatched within 24–48 hours of payment confirmation, excluding Sundays and public holidays.

Delivery time

Delivery typically takes 3–5 business days from dispatch. Remote pin codes may take longer. Every parcel is shipped tracked and insured through our logistics partner, Shiprocket, and a tracking number is shared by email once dispatched.

Failed delivery

Our courier partners make up to three delivery attempts. If all three are unsuccessful, the parcel is returned to origin. We will contact you to arrange redelivery; re-shipping charges may apply in that case.

Serviceability

We currently ship only within India. If your pin code is unserviceable we will contact you and issue a full refund.

Returns & Refunds

Eligibility

Unopened, factory-sealed flacons may be returned within 7 days of delivery. For hygiene and safety reasons, opened or used fragrance bottles cannot be returned or exchanged.

Damaged or incorrect items

If your parcel arrives damaged, leaking, or containing the wrong item, email photographs to info@scentobsessed.in within 48 hours of delivery. We will arrange a replacement or a full refund at no cost to you.

How to start a return

  • Email info@scentobsessed.in with your order number.
  • We will confirm eligibility and share return instructions within one business day.
  • Ship the sealed item back, or we will arrange a pickup where available.

Refund timeline

Once the returned item is received and inspected, approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. Payment gateway timelines may add a further 2–3 days for the amount to appear in your account.

Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund any time before dispatch. Email us as soon as possible with your order number.

Privacy Policy

We collect only what is required to fulfil your order: your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address. If you sign in with Google we receive your name and email address from Google. If you use the offer wheel we store the phone number you submit.

Payments

Payments are processed by Cashfree Payments. Your card, UPI, and banking details are entered on Cashfree's secure systems. We never see or store your full payment credentials.

Who we share with

We share your delivery details with Shiprocket and its courier partners solely to deliver your order, and with Cashfree solely to process payment. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to anyone.

Cookies

We use essential cookies and local storage to keep your cart and session working. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

You may request a copy of your data, a correction, or full deletion at any time by emailing info@scentobsessed.in. We will respond within 30 days. Note that order records required for tax and accounting purposes are retained as required by Indian law.
